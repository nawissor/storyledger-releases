# StoryLedger

**Every thread accounted for.**

StoryLedger is a story-bible and continuity app for Windows, built by novelist Iwan Ross for authors writing fiction — and the editors who have to keep it straight.

A character's eyes change colour between books. Someone knows about an event they were never present for. A clue introduced in chapter two quietly disappears. A place gets renamed halfway through a series.

StoryLedger gives those details somewhere to live.

> This repository contains the official StoryLedger installers and automatic update files. The application source code is not hosted here.

## What StoryLedger does

StoryLedger keeps the facts of a fictional world connected rather than scattered across documents, spreadsheets and notebooks.

### Continuity ledger
Record inconsistencies as you find them, assign severity and status, link the characters, places and events involved, and track each issue until it is resolved.

### Characters and relationships
Keep roles, descriptions, physical details and relationships together, including the continuity issues connected to each character.

### Story threads
Track mysteries, clues, secrets, promises and character arcs from introduction to payoff so that unresolved threads do not quietly disappear.

### Timeline
Record story events, backstory and historical dates, with statuses for events that are planned, confirmed or still need checking.

### Character Timeline Roadmap
See every character against every story event in story order. Distinguish between characters who were physically present and those who were only mentioned — useful when working out who could reasonably know what.

### Places and research
Keep setting details consistent and maintain research notes, sources and verification status alongside the story they belong to.

### Capture Inbox
Select text while working in another application and send it to StoryLedger with a keyboard shortcut, ready to organise later without interrupting the writing session.

### Export your Story Bible
Turn the information stored in StoryLedger into a clean Story Bible for use outside the app. Choose the sections you need and export them as Word, PDF or plain text for yourself, an editor, co-writer or other collaborator.

### Writing progress
Track word count against your own target and deadline. StoryLedger calculates the pace required from where you are now — without streaks, guilt or turning everything red because you took a week off.

## Built for long stories

StoryLedger works with individual novels as well as series.

Group books into a series and keep the people, places, chronology, research and continuity details that matter across multiple titles. It is particularly useful when book six suddenly needs to remember something established in book one.

Editors and proofreaders can also use StoryLedger as a searchable fiction style sheet, recording names, spellings, dates, physical details and inconsistencies for each manuscript or client.

## Private by design

Your stories stay on your computer.

- No account required
- No cloud library
- No subscription
- StoryLedger never opens or uploads your manuscript
- Your material is never used to train anything
- Automatic daily backups, with the latest fourteen retained
- Manual one-click backups wherever you choose
- Automatic application updates do not touch your library
