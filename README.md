# StoryLedger

**Every thread accounted for.** A story-bible and continuity app for Windows,
built by novelist Iwan Ross for authors writing series and the editors who work with them.

This repository holds the official installers and automatic updates.

- **Learn more and buy:** https://blurbcraftstudio.com/storyledger
- **Download the free 30-day trial:** [StoryLedger-win-Setup.exe](https://github.com/nawissor/storyledger-releases/releases/latest/download/StoryLedger-win-Setup.exe)

Windows 10 & 11 · works offline · your stories stay on your computer.
